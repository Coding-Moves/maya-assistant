About Maya

A quiet helper for busy chats.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Maya began with a familiar student problem: a quiz date is posted in a fast group chat, then disappears under a hundred new messages. She is a small personal assistant built to keep the useful things close.

What Maya does

With her owner's permission, Maya reads the WhatsApp groups she has joined. She can notice quizzes, assignments, exams, and deadlines, then send reminders only to her owner in a private chat. The owner can also ask what has been happening in a group, ask about saved tasks, and optionally connect a Google Calendar.

What she does not do

Maya does not post in groups, answer other people, or turn group messages into public content. She is not a replacement for official course notices, and she can make mistakes. Important dates should always be checked against the original source.

Who is behind it

Maya is an open-source experiment from Coding Moves Lab, where we try practical ideas in the open and are honest about their limits. You can see Maya's public project page on the Coding Moves website and inspect the code on GitHub.
