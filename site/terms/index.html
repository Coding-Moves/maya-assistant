Terms of use

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Maya is open-source software for personal use. These are the simple ground rules for using your own copy.

Your responsibilities

  • Add Maya only to WhatsApp groups where you have permission to do so.
  • Be open with group members if their messages may be processed by an AI service.
  • Keep your server, API keys, WhatsApp session, Calendar token, and any LMS credentials safe.
  • Check important deadlines against the official source. Maya can make mistakes.

Third-party services

Maya can connect to WhatsApp, Google Gemini, Google Calendar, and a host you choose. Each of those services has its own rules too. WhatsApp does not provide an official API for this way of reading groups on a personal account, so there is always a risk of restrictions on that account.

No warranty

Maya comes under the MIT License, without promises that every feature will always work or be correct. She may be unavailable, get something wrong, or stop working for a while. You are still responsible for checking deadlines and deciding what to do with her answers.
