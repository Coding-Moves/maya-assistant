Private by design

Your groups stay quiet. Your answers stay private.

Maya reads the groups you choose to add her to, but she never posts in them. Reminders and answers go only to her owner in a private chat.

How it works

A little help, exactly where you need it.

  1. 01

    Add Maya to your groups

    She can spot academic deadlines and keep ordinary group activity available for private questions.

  2. 02

    Ask her naturally

    Message Maya privately for what is due, what changed, or a quick catch-up on a group.

  3. 03

    Stay ahead gently

    Get morning and evening deadline reminders, optional Calendar events, and a weekly plan.
