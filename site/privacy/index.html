Maya Assistant

Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Maya is an open-source personal WhatsApp assistant. This policy describes the data handled when an owner runs their own Maya instance.

What Maya processes

Maya may process messages and supported media from WhatsApp groups that the owner deliberately adds Maya to, the owner’s private messages to Maya, task and reminder data, and optional VU Classic To Do or Google Calendar data.

When Gemini features are enabled, relevant group text and supported media are sent to Google Gemini to extract tasks or answer the owner’s private question. Maya is not intended for groups where participants have not agreed to this use.

How data is used

The data is used only to operate Maya: identifying deadlines, creating reminders, answering the owner privately, maintaining owner-approved memories, and optionally creating or reading Calendar events. Maya does not post in groups.

Where data is stored

Maya stores its database, WhatsApp session, and optional service credentials on the computer or server chosen by its owner. The project maintainer does not operate a shared Maya data service and does not receive a copy of an owner’s messages, tasks, credentials, or Calendar data through this website.

Google Calendar

Calendar access is optional and requires the owner’s Google consent. Maya requests permission to manage Calendar events so it can create, update, and remove events for Maya tasks and read upcoming events for private reminders. A local cache holds selected upcoming event details. When the owner asks Maya a Calendar-related question, a bounded selection of relevant cached event details may be included in a Google Gemini request to prepare the private answer. Maya does not use Google Calendar data for advertising or sell it. The owner can remove Calendar access at any time in their Google Account permissions.

Sharing and retention

Maya does not sell personal data or use it for advertising. Data may be shared with service providers selected by the owner, such as WhatsApp, Google Gemini, Google Calendar, and a hosting provider, only as needed to operate the owner’s instance. The owner controls retention and can delete the local database, revoke Google access, or remove Maya from WhatsApp groups.

Security

Owners are responsible for securing their server, environment file, backups, and device access. Keep API keys, WhatsApp session files, LMS credentials, and Calendar tokens private. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.
